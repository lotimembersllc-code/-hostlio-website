Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Hostlio Pro. Can't find an answer? Contact us.

What is Hostlio Pro?+
Hostlio Pro is an AI-powered hotel management platform for independent hotels, boutique B&Bs, and hostels. It automates guest messaging in 30+ languages, syncs your availability across 100+ OTA channels via Channex, and gives you a single dashboard to run your property.
How long does setup take?+
Most hotels are up and running in about 30 minutes. You connect your channels, import your rooms and reservations, and configure your AI assistant — no IT team required.
Which booking channels do you support?+
Through our Channex integration, Hostlio Pro connects to 100+ OTAs including Booking.com, Airbnb, Expedia, Agoda, and Trip.com. Availability and rates sync in real time to prevent overbookings. See our full channel list.
What languages does the AI support?+
The AI assistant replies to guests in 30+ languages automatically — it detects the guest's language and responds naturally, 24/7, with no extra effort from you.
Which messaging channels are supported?+
Hostlio Pro handles guest messages across OTA inboxes (Booking.com, Airbnb), Email, your website chat widget, and Telegram. More channels are added regularly.
How much does it cost?+
Early bird plans start at $49/month (Starter, normally $59) for up to 10 rooms. Pro ($89/mo) adds all channels plus transfer & tour upsells for up to 50 rooms. Growth ($149/mo) covers up to 150 rooms with priority support.
Is there a free trial?+
Yes — you can start a free trial and explore the full platform before committing. No credit card pressure, cancel anytime.
Do you offer online check-in?+
Yes. Guests can complete check-in online before arrival — providing their details and ID/passport documents through a secure form. You can review and export this information from your dashboard.
Is there a mobile app?+
Yes, Hostlio Pro includes a mobile app so you can manage reservations, guests, pricing, and messages on the go — even offline.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime from the billing portal.
